## Our Commitment
Techify is committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and safe workplace for all 600+ employees across our offices and remote locations. This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions, including Slack, email, video calls, and in-person settings.

## Expected Behavior
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect regardless of role, seniority, or department.
- Give and receive feedback professionally, especially during code review and design critique sessions.
- Disclose conflicts of interest (e.g., hiring or managing a family member) to People Ops before they arise where possible.

## Zero-Tolerance Harassment Policy
Techify maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind. This includes but is not limited to comments or conduct based on race, gender, religion, disability, or sexual orientation.

## Reporting
Concerns can be reported through any of the following channels:
- Directly to your manager or their manager
- People Ops (peopleops@techify.io)
- The anonymous Ethics Hotline (linked in the #hr-resources Slack channel), available 24/7

All reports are investigated within **5 business days** of submission, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is grounds for termination.

## Acknowledgment
All employees must complete the Code of Conduct training and sign an acknowledgment annually during their anniversary month, tracked via the Learning Management System (Lessonly).

## Consequences
Violations may result in coaching, formal warnings, or termination depending on severity, at the discretion of People Ops and department leadership.
