## System of Record
Salesforce is the single source of truth for all pipeline, accounts, and opportunity data at Techify. If it's not in Salesforce, it doesn't count for quota or commission purposes.

## Opportunity Stages
Deals move through: Prospecting → Discovery → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won/Closed Lost. Reps must update the opportunity stage within **24 hours** of any call or major status change.

## Lead Response Time
SDRs are expected to respond to new inbound leads within **1 business hour** during business hours, tracked via a Salesforce dashboard reviewed weekly by Sales Ops.

## Required Fields
Every opportunity must have a populated close date, deal amount, and next step before it can be marked "Commit" in forecast calls.

## Forecast Calls
AEs update forecast categories (Pipeline/Best Case/Commit/Closed) weekly ahead of the Thursday forecast call with their manager.

## Data Hygiene
Sales Ops runs a monthly data hygiene audit; reps with more than 10% of opportunities missing required fields may be flagged to their manager.

## Integrations
Salesforce is integrated with Gong (call recordings auto-attach to opportunities) and Outreach (email/call sequences sync activity back to the opportunity timeline automatically).

## Support
Questions about Salesforce fields, reports, or automation should go to #sales-ops-help; access issues should go through the standard IT access request process described in the VPN & Access Management guide.
