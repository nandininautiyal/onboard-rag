## Eligibility
All full-time employees are eligible for paid parental leave after 90 days of continuous employment, regardless of gender or family structure.

## Leave Duration
- **Primary caregivers** receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- **Secondary caregivers** receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement date, and can be split into two blocks with People Ops approval.

## Notice
Employees should notify their manager and People Ops at least 30 days in advance when the leave is foreseeable (e.g., a due date), though Techify recognizes this isn't always possible for adoption or foster placements.

## Pay & Benefits During Leave
Parental leave is paid at 100% of base salary and does not draw from the standard 18-day PTO bank described in the PTO policy. Health benefits continue uninterrupted during leave.

## Returning to Work
Employees may request a gradual return (e.g., part-time for the first 2 weeks back) by working with their manager and People Ops; this is evaluated case by case and is not guaranteed.

## Job Protection
Employees returning from parental leave are guaranteed their same or an equivalent role, consistent with applicable federal and state law.

## Contact
Questions about parental leave, including how it interacts with short-term disability in states where applicable, should go to peopleops@techify.io.
