## Company Holidays
Techify observes **11 paid company holidays** per year (e.g., New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving + the day after, and a winter break around the December holidays). The exact list is published annually in Workday under Company Resources.

## Benefits Enrollment Window
New hires have **30 days from their start date** to enroll in health, dental, and vision plans through Workday. Missing this window means waiting until the next Open Enrollment period (typically November) unless you have a qualifying life event.

## Health Plans
Techify offers 2 medical plan tiers (PPO and High-Deductible with HSA) through our benefits provider, along with standard dental and vision options.

## 401(k)
Techify offers a 401(k) plan with company match, with enrollment available anytime (not limited to the 30-day new hire window) since it's not health-plan-related.

## Life Events
Qualifying life events (marriage, birth/adoption of a child, loss of other coverage) allow benefits changes outside the standard windows; report these in Workday within 30 days of the event.

## Wellness Perks
Employees get an annual $500 wellness stipend (gym memberships, fitness classes, mental health apps) reimbursable through the standard Expense Reimbursement process.

## Questions
Benefits-specific questions go to peopleops@techify.io or #ask-peopleops; our benefits broker also holds monthly office hours announced in that channel.
