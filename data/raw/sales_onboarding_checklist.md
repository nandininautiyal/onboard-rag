## Welcome to the Team!
Whether you're joining as an SDR or an Account Executive, your first 30 days follow a structured ramp plan designed to get you productive fast.

## Week 1
- Complete IT and Okta setup (see IT Device Setup Guide)
- Get access to Salesforce, Gong, and Outreach
- Shadow at least 3 customer calls with a senior AE or CSM

## Weeks 2–3
- Complete Techify product certification quiz (must score 85%+ in the LMS)
- Shadow your first live discovery call
- Begin working assigned territory/accounts in Salesforce

## Week 4
- Lead your **first live customer call solo**, typically by day 14 for SDRs handling outbound, or by day 30 for AEs closing deals
- Meet with your manager for a 30-day check-in and ramp assessment

## Quota Ramp
New AEs are generally given a reduced quota (ramping quota) for their first 2 full quarters before hitting full quota in quarter 3. Exact ramp percentages are set individually by Sales Leadership per role level.

## Tools You'll Use
- **Salesforce** — system of record for all deals and accounts (see CRM Usage Guide)
- **Gong** — call recording and coaching
- **Outreach** — sequencing and outbound cadences
- **Slack** — #sales-wins, #sales-help, and your regional team channel

## Buddy Program
Every new sales hire is paired with a "sales buddy" from a different pod for their first 60 days, someone other than your direct manager, to ask "no dumb question is dumb" questions.
