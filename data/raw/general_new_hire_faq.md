## What happens before Day 1?
IT ships your laptop and provisions your accounts (email, Slack, Okta) so everything is ready before you start — see the Laptop & Device Setup Guide. Badge access to your office is also provisioned in advance for hybrid/in-office roles.

## What's on my first day?
- Company-wide orientation (led by People Ops), covering benefits, culture, and the Code of Conduct
- 1:1 with your manager to review your 30/60/90-day plan
- Meet your assigned onboarding buddy (a peer outside your direct reporting line)

## What's in my first week?
Most new hires complete IT setup, required compliance training in Lessonly (Code of Conduct, Data Security), and department-specific onboarding (e.g., the Sales Onboarding Checklist for new sales hires).

## How does badge access work?
Badge access is tied to your Okta identity and provisioned automatically for hybrid/in-office roles ahead of your start date. If your badge doesn't work on Day 1, visit the front desk or #it-help for a temporary badge.

## When do I enroll in benefits?
You have **30 days from your start date** to enroll in health, dental, and vision benefits in Workday; see the Benefits & Holidays FAQ for details.

## Who do I ask if I have a question that isn't covered here?
Start with your manager or onboarding buddy for anything role-specific, or #ask-peopleops for HR/benefits questions and #it-help for technical issues.

## When is my first paycheck?
Depends on when your start date falls relative to the bi-weekly payroll cycle — check the Payroll FAQ and Workday's Pay Calendar for exact dates.
