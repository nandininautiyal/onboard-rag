## When Do I Get Paid?
Techify runs payroll **bi-weekly**, with pay day falling every other Friday. You can view the exact schedule for the year in Workday under Pay > Pay Calendar.

## Direct Deposit
Direct deposit is required for all US-based employees. Funds are typically available in your account within 2 business days of the pay date, depending on your bank.

## Pay Stubs
Pay stubs are available in Workday under Pay > Payslips, typically posted the day before pay day.

## Tax Withholding
You can update your W-4 withholding elections anytime in Workday under Pay > Tax Documents; changes typically take effect on the next full pay cycle, not the one currently in progress.

## Commission & Bonus Payouts
Sales commission is calculated and paid out **monthly**, typically by the second pay day of the following month, based on closed-won deals in Salesforce for the prior month (see Commission structure guidance from your Sales Ops or manager for role-specific rates).

## On-Call Pay
Engineering on-call compensation (see On-Call Rotation Policy) appears as a separate line item on the pay stub for weeks with an active on-call shift.

## Paycheck Errors
If you notice an error in your paycheck, contact payroll@techify.io within 5 business days so it can be corrected in the next cycle; urgent off-cycle corrections are handled case by case.

## 401(k) & Benefits Deductions
401(k) contribution elections and changes made in Workday typically take 1-2 pay cycles to be reflected in your paycheck deductions.
