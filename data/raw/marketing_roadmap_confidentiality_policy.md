## What Is the Roadmap
The Techify product roadmap lives in Confluence under the Product space and outlines planned features, target quarters, and strategic themes for the next 2–3 quarters.

## Access
The roadmap is classified as **Internal** data (see Data Security & Classification Policy) and is accessible to all full-time employees by default, since we believe in transparency about where the product is headed.

## Confidentiality
Roadmap contents are strictly confidential and must never be shared externally — with customers, prospects, press, or on social media — without explicit written approval from the VP of Product, since specific dates and features are subject to change and could be misleading if shared prematurely.

## Sales Use
Account Executives may reference roadmap items in customer conversations only using pre-approved "directional" language from the Sales Enablement deck, never committing to a specific date. Because roadmap access itself requires manager approval via an access request ticket, reps should confirm they have current access before referencing it in a deal.

## Updates
Product Managers are expected to keep their area of the roadmap updated at least monthly; stale items (no update in 90+ days) may be flagged by the Product Ops team.

## NDA Considerations
Customers or prospects under an active mutual NDA may occasionally be shown specific roadmap slides in a sales context, but only with sign-off from the relevant Product Manager, logged in the deal's Salesforce notes.

## Questions
Reach out to #product-roadmap-questions for clarification on what can and can't be shared in a given customer conversation.
