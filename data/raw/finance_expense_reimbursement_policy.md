## Submission
All business expenses should be submitted through Concur within **30 days** of the expense being incurred. Expenses submitted after 60 days may be denied at Finance's discretion.

## Receipts
Receipts (photo or PDF) are required for any expense over **$25**. Expenses without required receipts may be rejected or require additional manager justification.

## Approval Chain
Expenses route automatically to your direct manager for approval, then to Finance for final processing. Manager approval should happen within 3 business days to keep the process moving.

## Reimbursement Timeline
Once fully approved, reimbursements are processed and paid out via direct deposit within **10 business days**.

## What's Reimbursable
- Client meals (within reasonable per-person limits — see Travel & Accommodation Policy for per diem specifics)
- Approved software/tools not available through standard IT procurement
- Conference and training expenses pre-approved by your manager
- Home office equipment beyond the standard IT stipend, case-by-case

## What's Not Reimbursable
- Personal entertainment unrelated to business purpose
- Commuting costs to your regular office
- Alcohol beyond a reasonable amount at approved client or team events (generally capped informally around 2 drinks per person, at manager discretion)

## Corporate Card
Employees with a recurring need for travel or client entertainment (primarily Sales and Executive roles) may be issued a corporate card, which still requires expense reports in Concur for reconciliation.
