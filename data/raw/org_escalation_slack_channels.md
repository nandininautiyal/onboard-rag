## Key Slack Channels
- **#it-help** — general IT support requests
- **#it-security-urgent** — suspected security incidents (24-hour reporting requirement, see Data Security Policy)
- **#ask-peopleops** — HR, benefits, and PTO questions
- **#eng-deploys** — deployment announcements and coordination (Tuesday/Thursday releases)
- **#eng-escalations** — where CSMs loop in on-call engineering for confirmed Sev 1 product bugs
- **#eng-breakglass** — logging break-glass access to production customer data
- **#sales-help** / **#sales-ops-help** — Salesforce and process questions for Sales
- **#product-launches** — internal launch announcements, at least 1 week before GA
- **#product-roadmap-questions** — what can/can't be shared externally from the roadmap

## General Escalation Path (Engineering)
IC Engineer → Engineering Manager → Director of Engineering → VP of Engineering → CTO. Most operational escalations (a stuck PR, a blocked deploy) should resolve at the EM level.

## General Escalation Path (Customer Issues)
CSM → CSM Lead → VP of Customer Success (for Sev 1 issues on top-20 accounts, the AE and VP of CS are also looped in automatically per the Customer Escalation Process).

## General Escalation Path (People/HR)
Employee → Manager → Director → VP of People / People Ops directly for sensitive matters (harassment, ethics concerns) that shouldn't go through a direct manager.

## When in Doubt
If you're not sure who to escalate to, posting in #ask-peopleops (for people questions) or #it-help (for anything technical) will get routed to the right person — you don't need to have the exact org chart memorized.
