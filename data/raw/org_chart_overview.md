## Company Snapshot
Techify was founded in 2016 and has grown to approximately **600 employees** across offices in San Jose (HQ), Austin, and Krakow, plus a distributed remote workforce.

## Top-Level Departments
- **Engineering** — organized into Platform, Product Engineering, and Infrastructure/SRE teams, led by the VP of Engineering
- **Product** — Product Managers organized by product area (Core Platform, Integrations, Analytics), led by the VP of Product
- **Design** — a centralized design team supporting Product, reporting to the VP of Product
- **Sales** — Account Executives and SDRs organized by segment (SMB, Mid-Market, Enterprise), led by the VP of Sales
- **Customer Success** — CSMs organized by segment mirroring Sales, led by the VP of Customer Success
- **Marketing** — Brand, Product Marketing, and Demand Gen, led by the VP of Marketing
- **Finance & Admin** — Accounting, FP&A, and Procurement, led by the CFO
- **People Ops** — HR, Recruiting, and Workplace, led by the VP of People

## Reporting Lines
Individual contributors report to a Manager or Engineering Manager (EM), who reports to a Director, who reports to a VP, who reports to the CEO. Most ICs are 3–4 levels from the CEO depending on team size.

## Cross-Functional Pods
Product areas are typically staffed as small cross-functional pods (1 PM, 1 Designer, 3–6 Engineers) rather than large siloed teams, to keep decision-making fast.

## Finding Team Structures
For a directory of who's on which team, see the "Team Directory" space in Confluence, kept up to date by each department's Ops/Chief of Staff function.
