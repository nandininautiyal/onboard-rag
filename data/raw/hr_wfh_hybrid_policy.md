## Hybrid Schedule
Techify operates on a hybrid model. All employees based within commuting distance of an office (San Jose HQ, Austin, or Krakow) are expected to work **in-office Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday**, with Monday and Friday available as remote days.

## Core Hours
Regardless of location, all employees should be reachable and online during core hours of **10:00 AM–4:00 PM local office time** to support cross-team collaboration and meetings.

## Fully Remote Roles
Some roles (e.g., certain Customer Success and Engineering positions filled outside commuting radius of an office) are designated fully remote at the time of offer. This designation is noted in the offer letter and cannot be changed unilaterally by the employee after starting.

## Temporary Remote Work
Employees may work remotely on a normally in-office day up to 4 times per month for personal reasons (appointments, travel, etc.) without formal approval, but should notify their manager in advance via Slack.

## Equipment for Remote Work
Home office equipment (monitor, keyboard, chair) can be requested through the IT device request process, up to a $400 one-time stipend per employee.

## Office Access
Badge access to office buildings is provisioned automatically for hybrid and in-office employees before their start date; see the New Hire FAQ for details.

## Policy Review
This policy is reviewed annually by People Ops and Department Heads and was last formally ratified in September 2025.
