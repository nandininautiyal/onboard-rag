## Launch Tiers
Not every feature gets the same treatment. We classify launches as:
- **Tier 1** — major launches (new product line, major pricing change) with full GTM campaign
- **Tier 2** — meaningful features with blog post, changelog, and sales enablement
- **Tier 3** — minor improvements, changelog entry only

## Readiness Review
For Tier 1 and Tier 2 launches, a Launch Readiness Review is held **2 weeks before GA (general availability)**, with sign-off required from Engineering, Product, Marketing, and Sales leadership.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **Product Manager** — owns the launch brief, feature scope, and success metrics
- **Marketing Manager** — owns messaging, campaign assets, and press/analyst outreach for Tier 1
- **Sales Enablement** — builds the sales deck and FAQ, delivered at least 1 week before GA
- **Engineering** — confirms the feature is behind a flag and rollout plan (per the Deployment & Release Process) is agreed with Product

## Internal Communication
All upcoming launches are tracked on the internal roadmap (see Roadmap & Confidentiality Policy) and announced in #product-launches at least 1 week ahead of GA for internal awareness, before any external announcement.

## Post-Launch
Marketing tracks adoption metrics for 30 days post-launch and shares a recap in the monthly GTM sync. Product Managers are responsible for a lightweight retro within 2 weeks of GA for Tier 1 launches.

## Embargoes
Tier 1 launches with press or analyst briefings are under embargo until the announced GA date/time; details must not be shared externally, including on personal social media, before that time.
