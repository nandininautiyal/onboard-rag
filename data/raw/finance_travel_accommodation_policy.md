## Booking Travel
All business travel (flights, hotels, rental cars) must be booked through **Concur Travel**, our approved booking tool, so trips are visible to Finance and for duty-of-care purposes.

## Flights
- Flights under 6 hours: economy class
- Flights 6+ hours: premium economy permitted with manager approval
- Business class requires VP-level approval regardless of flight length

## Hotels
Book hotels at or below the Concur-recommended nightly rate for the destination city where possible. Rooms above that guidance require a brief business justification note in Concur.

## Per Diem
Meals while traveling are reimbursed via a per diem of **$60/day** in the US (adjusted for higher-cost cities and international destinations per the Concur per diem table), rather than itemized receipts for every meal.

## Approval
Trips over $1,500 in estimated total cost require manager approval **before booking**; trips under that threshold can be booked directly but should still be shared with your manager.

## Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment follow the reimbursement rules in the Expense Reimbursement Policy, including the $25 receipt threshold.

## International Travel
Employees traveling internationally for work should notify People Ops at least 2 weeks in advance for visa, insurance, and safety-tracking purposes.

## Personal Travel Combined with Business
If you extend a business trip for personal travel, only the business-related portion of costs is reimbursable; you'll need to itemize this clearly in Concur.
