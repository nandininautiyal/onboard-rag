## Default Hardware
New hires are issued a MacBook Pro (14", M-series) by default. Employees needing a Windows machine (common for certain Sales Engineering and QA roles) can request one via an IT ticket, subject to manager approval.

## Setup Timeline
IT ships or hands off devices so they arrive **before your start date**, and initial setup (OS provisioning, MDM enrollment, account creation) is completed within your **first 2 days**.

## Required Software (Pre-installed)
- Okta (SSO login for all internal tools)
- Slack, Zoom, Google Workspace
- 1Password (company password manager)
- TechifyConnect VPN client (see VPN & Access Management guide)

## Mobile Device Management
All laptops are enrolled in Jamf MDM, which enforces disk encryption, screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity, and automatic security patching.

## Requesting Additional Hardware
Monitors, keyboards, docking stations, and home office equipment can be requested through the IT Help Desk portal (help.techify.io). Standard requests are fulfilled within 5 business days; the home office stipend cap is $400 as described in the Hybrid Work Policy.

## Loss or Damage
Report a lost or stolen device to IT Security **immediately** (within 1 hour if possible) so it can be remotely wiped. Report to #it-help in Slack or emergency line for after-hours incidents.

## Offboarding
Devices must be returned to IT within 5 business days of an employee's last day; remote employees are sent a prepaid shipping label.
